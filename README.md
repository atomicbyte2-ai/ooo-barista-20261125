# Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista

> Thank you for your email. I have left the inbox to become a barista — your message is now in a queue behind twelve oat lattes. I will reply once the foam settles, or not at all. Latte art over latency.

`main.ts` defines the entry line as a constant, validates it (one line, 1–280 characters, no newlines or control characters, no leading or trailing whitespace), and writes it with no trailing newline to `entry.txt` in the directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` by default), printing a short confirmation with the character count.
